Expression & motivation icons — alternatives

DECIDED 2026-09-02: expressions ship as C, generated geometry; motivations ship as B, monochrome marks in the same chip. C/D were ruled out for motivations because four family shapes × a filled/hollow split is eight slots for twelve items — the scheme provably cannot cover them, and separating the rest by dot count is the same failure as separating two silhouettes by sixteen pixels. Kept as the decision record.

Colour cannot identify these: expression colours are all cyan (worst pair ΔE 10.0) and motivation colours collide too (ΔE 7.2). So the glyph must carry identity and the disc only provides containment — the opposite of affinities, where colour is the whole message.

A keeps today's emoji and only adds the chip. B swaps to a monochrome geometric glyph in one neutral ink. C generates real vector glyphs — for expressions these are near-literal (push/pull/emit/draw are directional), for motivations they are family shape + member marker. D colours the disc by motivation family (4 families separate cleanly) with a monochrome glyph.
